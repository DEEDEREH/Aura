A Short Narrative about my favorite food!

As I enter the kitchen I smell the aroma of our homemade lasagna. As I gathered in the full smell I remembered the deep history of the dish and how it came into something real. But then I had to run t o go put on my shoes to get ready for our party. We were bringing lasagna to the party. I have always loved this dish for how long it lasts, the taste and for sure the smell.

As I take a bite, I feel a warm smooth sensation, it's warm and soft, it tastes like something right out of Italy, with a nice scent of tomato and the warm cheese make the lasagna taste amazing!. It smells like a new car but with tomato sauce and cheese layering the vehicle, as I conclude my bite as I look at the Lasagna I see the flat pasta noodles under the meat sauce and cheese in the shape of a rectangle missing its corner. I see the melted cheese dripping down the sides making the Lasagna look even more delightful! When I take a bite I can hear a slight crunch from the flat noodles! The lasagna is steaming and warm, as I take another bite the heat drowns up and the full taste gets released with that meat sauce and cheese slowly dissolving in my mouth! I think about how something this good was even thought of!

Lasagna first started in Ancient Greece, it started as a simple piece of thin dough. That was then baked and salted. We move onto Ancient Rome where they would use a thin sheet of dough and filling. So far in these time periods the recipe was really simple but as we go further .The lasagna recipe begins to progress, now we head into the middle ages in Italy where lasagna really started to come into fruition. In the 12th and 14th centuries Italians would use a thin piece of dough then boil it, then add grated cheese in between the thin piece dough and a green egg pasta sheet. In northern Italy they would do a similar thing with green egg pasta sheets, but they would also use a special sauce and greens. The name "Lasagna" which means a thin piece of dough. Both there's a second name, the plural way to say, Lasagne which means cooking pot but people eventually evolved to only Lasagna. In the Ancient times they never used tomatoes or spices. The recipes were really simple for easy use. But after time it evolved into what it is today. A few facts are, Lasagna was one Italy's original dishes and was one of the simplest dishes to make back then.

Me and my family which includes: My Mom, Brother in law, Sister and my Uncle. We were heading to a party for a college football game with the Oregon ducks and Boise state. It was the first game of the season so we decided to bring Lasagna as it is a family tradition. And bringing Lasagna would also make sense because of how many people it can serve. And there were around 25 people at the party. For the cooking process we used diced meat, Pasta sheets and cheese, and of course the tomato sauce. After the lasagna was finished we brought it down to my uncle's house where the game had already started. It was loud, dirty and dogs were running about. The score when we arrived was 7-3, I was holding the Lasagna and as I walked into the kitchen a brown and white dog jumped me almost knocking me over nut I was able to catch myself. I wondered if the dog was excited to see me or if it was just the food. Once I set down the lasagna someone already came up and grabbed a piece, then and there I new the lasagna was going to be a crowd favorite. I went over to the couch to enjoy the game then about an hour later I went up to grab some food and the Lasagna was already gone. And it was almost halftime so my sister and I went to Safeway and grabbed a premade one. Once we got back the room ecstatic the score 16-7, as I went to set down the lasagna they ducks scored once again leaving the room in utter chaos. I sat down the lasagna then also cheered, there was around 3 minutes left and some people were still hungry so they grabbed the rest of the Lasagna! After the game ended I wondered if I brought something else it would have been better; however, once I remembered how fast the guests ate the lasagna I knew it was the right choice. As we were heading home we understood why lasagna was brought to first of the season every year. I felt very good a fter because of how popular our lasagna really was and I think this will be a core memory because my entire family was there.

In conclusion, food isn't just about eating it, it's about bringing others together and enjoying the experience. To me Lasagna has always been a major part of our family as you have learned, but we don't just make it to eat, we make it to enjoy family time. And as the Lasagna begins to fade away the environment also begins to fade. What I see at the beginning during the cooking process is multiple ingredients to make something great, and by the end I get to witness wha t those ingredients made for everyone. That is my story on my chosen food. I really hope you enjoyed it!
